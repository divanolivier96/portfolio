PHILOSOPHY

In a sea of sameness, dare to be different.

For me, good design starts with curiosity: asking questions, finding the interesting detail, and figuring out how to tell a story in a way that feels right. I’m drawn to work with a clear idea and its own personality, whether that comes through in something quiet and considered or bold enough to stop you scrolling.

My taste sits somewhere between structure and experimentation. I love the clarity of Swiss modernism, the honesty of brutalism, and the rougher, more unexpected energy of grunge, deconstruction and surrealism. I enjoy bringing those influences together, finding the balance between a strong grid and a happy accident, simplicity and a little weirdness.

I don’t think every project needs the same look, or that design has to take itself too seriously to be thoughtful. The approach should fit the story, the people and the purpose behind it. Sometimes that means keeping things stripped back; sometimes it means breaking a few rules and seeing where the idea goes. Either way, I want the work to feel considered, human and a bit different from the usual.
