PHILOSOPHY

In a sea of sameness, dare to be different.

I’ve never believed good design should exist simply to fill space or follow what everyone else is doing. It should have a reason to exist, a clear point of view, and the confidence to be remembered. I’m drawn to ideas that feel considered rather than convenient, distinctive rather than familiar, and honest rather than dressed up for attention.

My visual instincts live somewhere between structure and disruption. I’m drawn to the discipline of Swiss modernism, the directness of brutalism, and the raw energy of grunge, deconstruction and surrealism. Grid and instinct. Clarity and character. I like the friction between them, and the unexpected ideas that can emerge when they’re allowed to meet.

I don’t believe in forcing one signature look onto every brief. The work should serve the idea, not my ego. Sometimes that means precision and restraint; sometimes it means breaking the grid and making a little noise. Whatever the direction, every choice should earn its place.
