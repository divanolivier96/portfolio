ABOUT

Ten years of design.

I'm Divan Olivier, a graphic designer based in Johannesburg, South Africa. I've spent ten years in graphic design and visual communication, working across print and digital.

I studied multimedia design and visual communication full-time for two years, then added training in photography and video editing. Photography and video sit alongside my design work when a project calls for them, but design is the craft I build everything around, from first idea to final file.

Outside client work I sketch on a drawing tablet, always looking for ways to expand my craft and bring more value to the people I work with.
